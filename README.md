# Quantum Computing Exploration

### Exploring the fundamentals of quantum computing through hands-on experimentation.

Welcome to my Quantum Computing Exploration repository!

Quantum computing is an emerging field that is reshaping how we think about computation, information processing and problem-solving.

This repository documents my learning journey into quantum computing through practical experiments, Python programming, quantum circuit simulations and explorations of emerging quantum technologies.

Rather than approaching quantum computing purely from a theoretical perspective, my goal is to understand its underlying principles by building, experimenting and documenting what I learn along the way.

## Objectives

- Understand the fundamental concepts behind quantum computing.
- Explore the differences between classical and quantum computing.
- Learn about qubits, superposition, entanglement and quantum measurement.
- Understand quantum gates and quantum circuits.
- Build and simulate quantum circuits using Python and Qiskit.
- Experiment with foundational quantum algorithms and states.
- Explore the potential applications and limitations of quantum computing.
- Gradually investigate access to real quantum computing hardware.

## Learning Roadmap

### 01. Foundations of Quantum Computing
- Classical bits vs. quantum bits
- Introduction to qubits
- Quantum states
- Superposition
- Quantum measurement
- Entanglement

### 02. Quantum Gates and Circuits
- Introduction to quantum gates
- Pauli-X, Y and Z gates
- Hadamard gate
- CNOT gate
- Quantum circuit construction
- Circuit visualization

### 03. Hands-on Quantum Experiments
- Building the first quantum circuit
- Single-qubit superposition experiments
- Two-qubit entanglement
- Bell State experiments
- Measurement and probability distributions
- Three-qubit GHZ State experiments

### 04. Quantum Algorithms
- Introduction to quantum algorithms
- Deutsch-Jozsa algorithm
- Grover's search algorithm
- Quantum Fourier Transform
- Exploring how quantum algorithms differ from classical approaches

### 05. Quantum Simulation and Hardware
- Simulating quantum circuits locally
- Understanding noise in quantum systems
- Ideal vs. noisy simulations
- Exploring IBM Quantum hardware
- Running experiments on real quantum processors

### 06. Emerging Applications
- Quantum cryptography
- Quantum communication
- Quantum chemistry and material science
- Quantum optimization
- Quantum machine learning
- Potential intersections between quantum computing and artificial intelligence
